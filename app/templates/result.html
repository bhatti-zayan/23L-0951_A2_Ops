Please read before relying on this

VisionGuard is a statistical model trained on fewer than 1,000 web images. On its test set it was right about 4 times out of 5. It is most often wrong on digital art, painted or heavily edited images, photorealistic AI portraits and images from newer generators.

We tuned it to avoid wrongly calling real photos "AI", so it misses some AI images instead. "Uncertain" means the model leans towards AI but is not confident enough to say so. Treat the result as a hint, not proof, and never use it alone to accuse anyone of faking an image.
